# Screenshot credits

The README's screenshots in this folder show Pindu launcher with a demo library of 20 free and open-source games.
Their art comes only from libre sources:

- **Covers and banners** are crops of each game's official screenshots as published in its Flathub (AppStream)
  listing, under the licence given there. Each cover has its head darkened under the game's name, set in
  [Adwaita Sans](https://gitlab.gnome.org/GNOME/adwaita-fonts) Bold (SIL Open Font License 1.1), except Shattered
  Pixel Dungeon's, a crop of its title screen whose own logo spells the name.
- **Icons** are each game's AppStream icon from the same listing.
- **Logos** (SuperTuxKart, Battle for Wesnoth, Mindustry) are the official logo files from each game's own
  repository, under the terms listed below.
- **Names, developers and licences** come from the games' AppStream data; the short descriptions are their AppStream
  summaries (AppStream metadata is CC0-1.0). Categories and release years are the demo's own picks, and playtimes,
  play dates, collections and favourites are made up.

The launcher icons in the sidebar and on the covers are Pindu launcher's own files, credited in
[`data/icons/sources/LICENSE`](../../data/icons/sources/LICENSE). The store screenshots in `data/screenshots/` have
their own credits in [`data/screenshots/CREDITS.md`](../../data/screenshots/CREDITS.md).
